# Voice profile — <your name> (template)

Copy this file, fill it with your own rules, and point callus at it with
`CALLUS_PROFILE=path/to/this.md` or `--profile path/to/this.md`. The judge
reads these rules verbatim, so be concrete and specific. See
[voz_victor.md](voz_victor.md) for a filled-in example.

- **Framing you prefer:** (e.g. positive — describe what a thing IS, not what
  it is not; first-person situational hooks; concrete numbers over vague
  magnitudes)
- **Words / phrases you avoid:** (e.g. corporate filler, comparisons against
  competitors, defensive clarifications)
- **Titles / facts to respect:** (e.g. a degree you do/don't hold, a company
  name, how you sign off)
- **Verb precision rules:** (words you only use under a specific condition)
- **Personal tells that must NOT be penalized:** (habits a generic detector
  would flag but that are genuinely yours — colloquialisms, a recurring
  closing line, organic typos on non-technical words)
- **Pronoun rules:** (e.g. 'I/my' for personal introspection, 'we/our' for
  team or company capacity)
- **Hooks you like:** (an opener that sounds like you)
- **Hooks you avoid:** (aphoristic imperatives, hinge phrases, atemporal
  generalizations)
